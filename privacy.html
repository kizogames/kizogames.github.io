Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Kizo Games handles information through this informational website. It does not automatically cover future games or applications, which may have their own privacy notices.

Information we receive

Our website does not include accounts, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or a contact form in this version. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message.

Cookies and browser storage

This version does not set cookies or use local storage for analytics, advertising, profiles, or preferences. The navigation menu and game details work without saving visitor information.

How we use information

We use email correspondence to respond to enquiries, discuss potential collaborations, and manage business communications.

Hosting and technical data

The website is designed for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub and network providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and access logs under their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Third-party resources

Fonts, artwork, styles, and scripts are served with this website. This version does not embed videos or load third-party analytics or advertising. Clicking an external link or an email link opens the relevant provider, whose own privacy practices apply.

Retention and rights

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant enquiry and applicable business requirements. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us, subject to applicable legal obligations.
