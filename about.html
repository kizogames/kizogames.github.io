About Kizo Games

Small studio.
Big curiosity.

Kizo Games is an independent game development studio based in Casablanca, Morocco. We focus on approachable casual games for web and mobile, while exploring creative software concepts.

💡

Our mission

Create accessible, delightful digital experiences with clear ideas, polished interactions, and a playful personality.

🛠️

Our process

We start with an idea, prototype core interactions, evaluate usability, and refine projects through iteration.

🌍

Our direction

Build a sustainable portfolio of original games and practical software for audiences across digital platforms.

How we build

We begin with a simple playable idea, refine the core interactions, and develop it through prototyping and playtesting.
